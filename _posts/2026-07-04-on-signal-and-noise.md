title: On Signal and Noise
---
A cybernetic symphony is not a contradiction. Feedback is the oldest kind of
music: a system listening to itself, adjusting, listening again. Every thermostat
is a small composer, holding one note against the weather.

We tend to hear noise as the enemy of signal, but noise is what lets a system
know it is still alive. A perfectly silent channel carries nothing. The trick is
not to eliminate noise but to learn its grammar — to know which fluctuations mean
*change ahead* and which are just the room breathing.

I think a lot of writing works this way too. You put a sentence down, read it
back, and some small internal governor tells you it's off. You adjust. You read
again. The loop is the whole craft; the finished piece is just where you decided
to stop iterating.

So this blog is a feedback loop pointed outward. Notes, corrections, second
thoughts. Signal when I can manage it, honest noise the rest of the time.
